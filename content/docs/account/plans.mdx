## Why moderation is split

The actions themselves — warn, timeout, kick, ban — are **free**. A server that
cannot remove a spammer will not stay installed long enough to upgrade.

What Pro sells is the layer on top: automod, raid protection, the channel tools, and
the searchable player record log. Cases are written on every tier, so a server that
upgrades finds its history already there rather than starting from nothing.

Duty tracking is free for the same reason: every paid analytics feature is built from
the sessions it records.

## Downgrading never deletes anything

If you drop from Pro to Free with 12 ticket categories, all 12 stay and keep working.
You are blocked from creating a 13th until you are back under the limit.

The same applies everywhere: quotas, panels, records, levelling XP, infractions. They
stop being *acted on*, and they are still there when you come back.

<Callout title="There is no trial">
  The free tier is the trial. Layering a fortnight of Pro on top of a tier that is
  already usable only created a cliff two weeks after installing.
</Callout>

## When a payment fails

Paid features keep working for **three days** after a failed payment. A declined card
should not take down your ticket system mid-shift. After that the server drops to
free, keeping all its data.

## Feature not working?

In order of likelihood:

1. **The module is off** — `/modules view`. A switched-off module is silent by design.
2. **Your plan does not include it** — the command will say so and name the tier.
3. **Braven is missing a permission** — it will tell you which one, in which channel.
