Braven has 63 commands. You will use about six of them.

This page is generated from the bot's own command definitions, so it is always
exactly what is registered in your server — not a list somebody forgot to update.

<Callout title="Not seeing a command?">
  Three reasons, in order of likelihood: the **module is switched off**
  (`/modules view`), your **plan does not include it** (`/plan`), or Discord has
  not finished syncing — new commands can take up to an hour to appear globally.
</Callout>

Most commands need **Manage Server**. The exceptions are the ones members are
meant to use: `/suggest`, `/rank`, `/xp`, `/birthday set`, `/checksits`,
`/staff upvote` and the ticket buttons themselves.
