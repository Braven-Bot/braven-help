---
title: Quick start
description: Invite Braven, point it at a channel, and post a working ticket panel in about five minutes.
---

Three steps gets you a working ticket system. Everything else is optional.

## 1. Invite Braven

Use the **Add to Discord** button on [bravenbot.com](https://bravenbot.com). Accept the
permissions it asks for — [what each one is for](/docs/getting-started/permissions) —
and pick the server.

Braven does not need Administrator. If you are being offered it, you are looking at
the wrong invite link.

## 2. Tell it which channel tickets belong in

In your server:

```
/setup channel channel:#support
```

That is the channel tickets open under. Tickets are **private threads** on it, so
members only ever see their own, and your staff see all of them without you managing
permissions per ticket.

Two more worth doing now:

```
/setup staff-add role:@Staff
/setup transcripts channel:#ticket-logs
```

The first decides who can see and claim tickets. The second is where a transcript
gets posted when a ticket closes.

<Callout title="No staff role yet?">
  Anyone with **Manage Threads** on the channel counts as staff even with no role
  configured, so you are not locked out while you set things up.
</Callout>

## 3. Build a panel

A panel is the message members click. Build it in the dashboard:

**[bravenbot.com/dashboard](https://bravenbot.com/dashboard) → your server → Ticket
panels → New panel**

Give it a channel and a title, add at least one category, then **Post**. You get a
live preview of the embed as you build it, so nothing is a surprise.

Prefer commands? These do the same thing:

```
/panel create channel:#support title:Support
/panel category panel:1 label:General Support
/panel post panel:1
```

That is a working ticket system. A member clicks, a thread opens, your staff claim
it and close it, and the transcript lands in your log channel.
