<Callout>This is a **Pro** feature. `/subscribe` to enable it.</Callout>

Tebex sends a webhook on payment, and Braven records the transaction and can grant a
role for it.

`/tebex lookup` checks a transaction ID, which is how you answer "I paid and did not
get my perks" without leaving Discord.

Chargebacks are recorded too, so a refunded donation does not leave someone holding a
role they no longer paid for.

## Commands

### `/tebex`

Look up and verify Tebex payments

| Subcommand | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/tebex setup` | Get the webhook URL for your Tebex dashboard |
| `/tebex lookup` | Staff: look up a transaction |
| `/tebex verify` | Claim a payment you made |
