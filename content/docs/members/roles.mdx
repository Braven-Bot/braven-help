title: Roles
description: "Auto roles on join, sticky roles, self-assignable panels, and delegated granting."
---

Four different ways of deciding who holds which role.

**Auto roles** are given when somebody joins. Free.

**Sticky roles** come back if a member leaves and rejoins — so leaving does not shed
a mute or a blacklist. Pro.

**Self-assignable roles** are panels members use to pick their own roles, with
buttons or a dropdown. Free.

**Delegated granting** lets a role hand out specific other roles without giving
anyone Manage Roles. Pro.

<Callout title="Braven's role must sit above the roles it grants">
  Discord will not let any bot manage a role above its own, whatever its permissions.
  If roles are not being applied, check the hierarchy first.
</Callout>

## Commands

### `/autoroles`

Roles given automatically when someone joins

| Subcommand | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/autoroles create` | Give a role to everyone who joins from now on |
| `/autoroles delete` | Stop giving a role on join |
| `/autoroles list` | Show the roles given on join |

### `/stickyroles`

Roles that come back when a member rejoins

| Subcommand | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/stickyroles create` | Make a role stick to members who leave and return |
| `/stickyroles delete` | Stop a role from being reapplied, and forget who held it |
| `/stickyroles list` | Show sticky roles and how many members are remembered |

### `/selfroles`

Panels members use to pick their own roles

| Subcommand | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/selfroles create` | Start a new panel |
| `/selfroles option-add` | Add a role to a panel |
| `/selfroles option-remove` | Take a role off a panel |
| `/selfroles post` | Post the panel, or repost it after changes |
| `/selfroles list` | Show this server’s panels |
| `/selfroles delete` | Delete a panel |

### `/rolemanager`

Let a role hand out other roles

| Subcommand | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/rolemanager modify` | Give a role permission to hand out roles |
| `/rolemanager remove` | Take the power away again |
| `/rolemanager list` | Who can hand out roles, and who gave them that |

### `/role`

Give or take a role you have been trusted with

| Subcommand | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/role add` | Give someone a role |
| `/role remove` | Take a role away from someone |
