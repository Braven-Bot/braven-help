---
title: Welcome messages
description: "Greet new members, and thank the ones who boost."
---

Welcome messages are free. Booster replies are Pro.

Both support placeholders like `{user}`, `{server}` and `{count}`, so a message can
name the person and the server it is in.

Members held by [raid protection](/docs/moderation/raid) are not welcomed until they
are let through — otherwise a raid produces a wall of greetings for accounts you are
about to remove.

## Commands

### `/welcome`

Greet new members

| Subcommand | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/welcome channel` | Post a welcome in a channel |
| `/welcome dm` | Send a welcome by direct message |
| `/welcome disable` | Stop welcoming members |
| `/welcome test` | Show what your welcome looks like, using you |
| `/welcome show` | Show the current settings and the placeholders |

### `/booster`

Thank members who boost the server

| Subcommand | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/booster set` | Reply when someone boosts |
| `/booster disable` | Stop replying to boosts |
